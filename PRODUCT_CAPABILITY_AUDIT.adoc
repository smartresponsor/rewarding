= Product Capability Audit: Rewarding
:revdate: 2026-09-20

== Boundary verdict

Rewarding owns non-monetary loyalty points, tiers, earn/redeem rules, expiry, reversals and benefits. Money-like store credit remains Walleting.

== Capability matrix

[cols="3,1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Capability |Status |Audit
|Reward account/reference |IMPLEMENTED |Immutable account entity is mapped to the Rewarding data persistence surface.
|Immutable points ledger |IMPLEMENTED |Mapped immutable transaction entity, durable DBAL repository, account-scoped ledger version, idempotency uniqueness, and initial migration are implemented.
|Earn |IMPLEMENTED |Idempotent durable ledger credit implemented; rule selection remains M3.
|Redeem |IMPLEMENTED |Idempotent durable debit with optimistic version contract and insufficient-balance guard implemented.
|Expire |PARTIAL |Replay-safe durable expiry debit implemented; scheduler/source-lot policy remains M3.
|Adjust/reverse |IMPLEMENTED |Durable append-only adjustment and linked reversal implemented.
|Balance derivation |IMPLEMENTED |Balance is derived from immutable ledger entries.
|Earn/redeem rules |MISSING |Versioned effective rules.
|Tiers/status progression |MISSING |Modern loyalty baseline.
|Benefits |MISSING |Eligibility output; Promoting may consume incentive facts.
|Redemption references |MISSING |Reference order/cart without owning them.
|Store credit |NOT_APPLICABLE |Explicitly Walleting, not Rewarding.
|===

== Milestone direction

M1: RewardAccountEntity + immutable RewardTransactionEntity ledger — implemented with Doctrine mapping, DBAL repository, and initial PostgreSQL migration.
M2: earn/redeem/expire/adjust/reverse with idempotency — implemented for the explicit ledger-operation slice; expiry scheduling/source-lot policy remains M3.
M3: rules, tiers and benefits.
M4: Walleting boundary acceptance and commerce integration.
